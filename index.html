01 Dossier · Who we are read-time: 40 seconds, or 1 period

A section is just a room
until it isn't.

We are named after Stephen Hawking, the physicist who never let limits decide what he could figure out. Fitting for a room of future engineers and architects under the Academic Track: fifty-one strong, and officially the loudest room in the building.

This is our section's website: the roster, the officers, the timeline, the evidence, and a guestbook for anyone who spent even one period next to us. It is not official. It has never been so official.
